Why does water appear in a garage beside a retaining wall after rain?

Water at a garage wall can follow soil moisture, surface runoff, drainage defects or a wall junction. The visible damp patch alone cannot establish the source or whether pressure is acting behind the wall. The Ellis team reviews the rain pattern and accessible wall and drainage details during an on-site assessment, then confirms the repair plan and written quote.

Can a retaining wall be waterproofed from the inside?

The repair side and method depend on how the wall was built and whether the soil-facing surface can be reached. An internal coating is not automatically equivalent to addressing water on the retained side. Where excavation, drainage design or structural movement is involved, the relevant specialist input should be included in the agreed scope.
