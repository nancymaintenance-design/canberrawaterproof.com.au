Why does water appear in a garage beside a retaining wall after rain?

Water at a garage wall can follow soil moisture, surface runoff, drainage defects or a wall junction. The visible damp patch alone cannot establish the source or whether pressure is acting behind the wall. Can a retaining wall be waterproofed from the inside?

The repair side and method depend on how the wall was built and whether the soil-facing surface can be reached. An internal coating is not automatically equivalent to addressing water on the retained side.
