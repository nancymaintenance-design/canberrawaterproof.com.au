Why does water appear in a garage beside a retaining wall after rain?

Water at a garage wall can follow soil moisture, surface runoff, drainage defects or a wall junction. The visible damp patch alone cannot establish the source or whether pressure is acting behind the wall. MEL ONE arranges an on-site assessment of the rain pattern and accessible wall and drainage details, identifies the water-entry cause and confirms the repair plan and written quote.

Can a retaining wall be waterproofed from the inside?

The repair side and method depend on how the wall was built and whether the soil-facing surface can be reached. An internal coating is not automatically equivalent to addressing water on the retained side. Where excavation, drainage design or structural movement is involved, MEL ONE arranges the appropriate qualified specialists and agrees any further access, their work and the written quote before proceeding.
