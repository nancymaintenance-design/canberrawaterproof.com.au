Laundry silicone selection: mould resistance, water sealing and durability

For suitable laundry tub edges, tiled corners and perimeter joints, we usually consider a mould-resistant, sanitary-grade silicone sealant compatible with the adjoining surfaces. A suitable neutral-cure formulation can provide flexible adhesion to common wet-area finishes. We confirm the particular product against the tile, metal, painted surface or other substrate before specifying it.

Mould-resistant formulations help resist fungal growth on the sealant in humid areas. Ventilation, drying and regular cleaning support that performance by limiting retained moisture and residue. We also investigate recurring dampness so the source of moisture is addressed alongside the joint repair.

Once fully cured, a correctly prepared and bonded silicone joint provides a flexible water seal at the treated junction. Elasticity helps accommodate normal movement between adjoining finishes. For joints exposed to sunlight or outdoor conditions, we check the product’s documented UV, weathering and ageing resistance, as well as its adhesion and movement capability, to suit the exposure.
