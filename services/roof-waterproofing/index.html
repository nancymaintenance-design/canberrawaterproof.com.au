Roof leak inspection and treatment boundaries

The inspection considers accessible membrane seams, flashing interfaces, penetration bases and outlets against the room and weather reported. Water can travel from a roof-to-wall junction before it appears inside, so the internal stain is only one clue.

A local membrane or junction repair needs a compatible, sound surrounding system. Wider deterioration may require membrane renewal. Roof painting does not repair a failed flashing or penetration; roof-covering replacement, gutter alterations and separately licensed roofing or drainage work are listed apart from waterproofing.

Sheet membrane benefits for suitable roofs

For a suitable flat or low-pitched roof, factory-made sheet membranes provide a specified nominal thickness across each roll. Reinforced modified-bitumen sheets add tensile strength, while the planned sheet layout makes laps and terminations accessible for inspection. These features support consistent coverage over larger roof areas when the substrate, bonding and drainage details are properly prepared.

For an exposed roof, we select the complete weathering system, including a cap sheet or protective finish documented for UV exposure. A mineral-faced APP modified-bitumen cap sheet is one option, with maintenance access suited to that system. Selection also considers roof movement, existing coatings, outlets and any need for a base sheet or vapour-control layer.

Roof sheet membrane preparation, laps and terminations

We assess the existing surface, remove loose material and agree repairs, moisture conditions and drainage falls before the specified primer and membrane build-up. The installation scope identifies the layer arrangement, lap layout, upstands, outlet connections and penetration flashings. For a suitable torch-applied bituminous system, controlled heating bonds the sheet and seals its laps, with specialist hot-work and fire precautions planned for the roof construction. Inspection covers the bonded areas, lap seals and terminations before handover and ongoing maintenance.

PVC and TPO roof sheets are separate system options, commonly using hot-air-welded seams and their own fixing and detailing requirements. Where roof construction calls for a different installation method, we assess a suitable self-adhesive, mechanically fixed or liquid-applied system. The written quote records the selected product, installation method, protection and any agreed testing.

Roof leaks during heavy rain

Wind-driven rain, overflowing gutters, blocked outlets and a vulnerable flashing or wall junction can each change where water enters. A stain inside the building does not always sit directly below the entry point. Record the weather, affected room and any safe exterior photographs before arranging a roof leak assessment.

Roof painting and waterproofing system differences

A coating described as roof paint is not automatically a repair for a failed flashing, penetration or waterproofing membrane. The existing roof assembly and the confirmed water path determine the work scope. If a roof-covering repair needs a licensed roofer, the quote lists that task separately from the Ellis team’s waterproofing work.

Waterproofing substrate preparation

For exposed roofs, we usually assess a weather- and UV-suitable liquid-applied system or a roof-specific sheet membrane against the existing roof assembly. The options can include a polyurethane system with its specified protective topcoat, an APP modified-bitumen roof system, or a suitable PVC or TPO roof membrane. Substrate condition, falls, outlets, movement, permitted traffic and maintenance access guide the selection and written scope.

Roof preparation usually involves cleaning, repairs and the priming or separation layers specified for the build-up. Sheet systems use their nominated fixing, seams, corners and terminations; hot-air welding, torch application and self-adhesive installation each follow their own system instructions and safety arrangements. Liquid systems have specified film and curing checks. ARDEX WPM 185 and ARDEX WPM 300 illustrate roof cap-sheet and epoxy primer/barrier roles respectively.
