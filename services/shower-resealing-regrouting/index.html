Shower grout and silicone repair suitability

We check missing grout between tiles, split silicone at changes of plane, screen junctions and signs of tile movement. Old silicone, residue and damp joint surfaces affect preparation and the bond of the replacement sealant.

Grout fills tile joints; flexible sealant serves movement joints. Neither treatment repairs a concealed membrane failure or a plumbing leak. Persistent moisture after a previous surface repair needs reassessment before more grout or silicone is applied.

Grout, sealant and bond breakers are not interchangeable

Grout fills appropriate fixed tile joints. Flexible sealants and bond breakers form part of specified movement-joint or membrane details; they are not substitutes for grout across a tiled floor. Product compatibility and the condition found on site determine the choice.
