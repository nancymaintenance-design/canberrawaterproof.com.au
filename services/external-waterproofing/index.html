External water ingress inspection locations

We consider the window perimeter, door threshold, wall-base junctions and accessible external joints alongside internal staining. Wind-driven rain, water pooling against a wall and persistent moisture when the weather is dry can indicate different causes.

Local joint sealing may suit an accessible isolated gap. Failed wall details or concealed interfaces may need opening and reinstatement; coating an internal stain does not establish or stop the source. Drainage changes, structural faults and window replacement are separate tasks where required.

Rainwater ingress and condensation assessment

Moisture that follows wind-driven rain may involve an external junction, window detail or drainage path. Condensation can relate to humidity and cold surfaces.
