Can just the shower be waterproofed and retiled?

Sometimes a shower-only scope is feasible. The decision depends on the substrate, adjoining waterproofing details and how the repaired area can connect to the surrounding construction. Explain which fittings and finishes you want to retain so the assessment can establish the required access. A local scope still needs to address the affected waterproofing system.

What if I have already bought tiles for my ensuite?

Send the tile specifications, sizes and available quantity when discussing the work. Suitability, layout, substrate condition and compatibility with the proposed installation need review before owner-supplied materials can be included. Ask the quote to identify who supplies tiles and other materials, and what happens if additional preparation or tiles are needed.

How should work be planned when we have only one bathroom?

Tell MEL ONE before agreeing the scope. Ask which facilities will be unavailable and when access can resume, allowing for preparation, membrane and finishing requirements. The actual system and site conditions determine the sequence; arrange alternative washing facilities around the confirmed plan. Use the waterproofing and retiling quote guide to request separate removal, substrate, membrane, tiling, finishing and waste-removal items, then discuss your bathroom requirements.

Bathroom waterproofing that addresses the failed wet area, not just the visible symptom

Water staining, a leaking shower, loose tiles or failed silicone can be signs of a deeper wet-area issue. MEL ONE assesses the bathroom as a whole, including the shower base, wall-floor junctions, tiled finishes and nearby surfaces, before recommending the most suitable repair path for the property.

Where the waterproofing system needs renewal, the service can bring removal, substrate preparation, waterproofing membrane work, retiling and finishing work into one written scope. This gives you a clear view of what the bathroom repair includes before work starts.

What the bathroom waterproofing service can include

Your written scope is tailored to the bathroom and the condition found on site. Depending on the repair required, MEL ONE can include the following work as clearly listed items:

  • Removal of affected tiles, fittings or finishes where access to the waterproofing area is required.
  • Preparation and repair of the substrate before a new wet-area membrane system is applied.
  • Waterproofing membrane work to the agreed bathroom, shower or floor-and-wall junction areas.
  • Retiling, grout, silicone and agreed finishing work to return the bathroom to a practical finished condition.

A straightforward process from assessment to finished bathroom

  1. Tell us what you are seeing. Call us with the Canberra suburb and the bathroom area affected or planned renovation. You can contact us without photographs or measurements.
  2. We define the repair scope. MEL ONE inspects and measures the wet area on site, checks the leak cause or renovation requirements and confirms waterproofing, access and finishing work in the work plan and written quote.
  3. We complete the agreed work. The repair is delivered to the written scope, with waterproofing and retiling work coordinated as required for the bathroom.
