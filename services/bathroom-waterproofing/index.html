When to get it checked

Water staining, moisture beside a wet area, failed sealant, loose finishes or rainwater entry can each have different causes. Visible symptoms alone do not confirm the failed layer or the right repair method.

What the service includes

The agreed scope identifies the affected area, access, preparation, waterproofing or sealing work, finishing tasks and any coordination required. Inclusions are confirmed before work starts.

Repair options and limitations

Targeted resealing is used for specified joints or edges. Where water entry relates to a deeper assembly, access, membrane replacement or retiling may be needed. Plumbing, drainage and roof issues are directed to the appropriate trade where applicable.

What affects a quote

Area, access, removal work, substrate condition, materials, drainage details, tile supply, finishing work and waste removal affect scope and price. A membrane-only price is not the same as a complete removal and retiling project.

Next step

Send your suburb, a description of the affected area and photos if available. Photos help the initial discussion but do not replace assessment.

Laundry waterproofing context

Signs to record

Persistent moisture, failed finishes, renovation work or known access beneath tiles are common reasons to discuss a full wet-area scope.

What MEL ONE includes in the agreed scope

Tile removal and substrate preparation, waterproofing membrane work, retiling and agreed finishing work are set out as separate inclusions.

Service boundaries

A waterproofing scope does not treat plumbing disconnection, fixture work or tile supply as automatically included. Each item is listed in the written scope.

Quote factors

Bathroom size, tile removal, substrate repairs, membrane system, tile selection, access, waste removal and finishing details.
