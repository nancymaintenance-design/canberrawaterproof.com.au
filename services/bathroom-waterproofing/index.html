Bathroom waterproofing scope

Your written scope is tailored to the bathroom and the condition found on site. Depending on the repair required, MEL ONE can include the following work as clearly listed items:

  • Removal of affected tiles, fittings or finishes where access to the waterproofing area is required.
  • Preparation and repair of the substrate before a new wet-area membrane system is applied.
  • Waterproofing membrane work to the agreed bathroom, shower or floor-and-wall junction areas.
  • Retiling, grout, silicone and agreed finishing work to return the bathroom to a practical finished condition.

Substrate checks before membrane installation

For bathroom floors and shower walls, we usually consider a flexible liquid-applied membrane or a dedicated under-tile sheet system suited to the substrate and tile finish. Polymer-modified cementitious products are considered where the specific system documents that wet-area use. Our preparation covers cleaning, repairs, levelling and the moisture conditions required by the selected system.

Membrane curing, tiling and shower reuse

Bathroom waterproofing details usually include the specified primer, wall-to-floor junction treatment, pipe collars and connections to floor wastes and waterstops. Liquid systems are checked for the specified coverage and film thickness; sheet systems have their own seam and corner details.
