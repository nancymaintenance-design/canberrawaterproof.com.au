How MEL ONE works

From on-site assessment to an agreed repair plan.

01

Document the affected area

Share the location, what you can see and when it occurs. We inspect the affected area on site and confirm the repair plan and written quote. Photos help us prepare when available.

02

Inspect accessible details

MEL ONE inspects accessible junctions, finishes and surrounding conditions on site to identify the water-entry cause and repair requirements.

03

Confirm the next step

The Ellis team assesses accessible details on site, explains the repair plan and confirms a written quote. The scope records the work our team will complete, access requirements and any separately licensed task.
