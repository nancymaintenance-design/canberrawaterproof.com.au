Bathroom Waterproofing and Retiling Quote in Canberra: What Should It Include?

The Ellis team assesses Canberra properties on site and provides a written waterproofing scope covering preparation, materials, finishes and handover.

How should you compare bathroom waterproofing and retiling quotes?

A waterproofing and retiling quote should distinguish inspection, removal, substrate preparation and falls, membrane details, curing and agreed tests, tile supply and laying, flexible joints, reinstatement, waste and related trades. MEL ONE plans removal, preparation, waterproofing, retiling and finishing as an agreed scope for your Canberra property.

The total is useful only when you know what it covers. A membrane-only quotation and a complete bathroom strip-out and rebuild describe different jobs. For wet-area renewal, see our bathroom waterproofing and retiling service; external drainage, thresholds and access also affect a balcony waterproofing scope.

What should be written into the quote?

Ask each provider to mark the following items as included, separately priced or awaiting inspection. “Waterproofing and tiling” as one line leaves too much room for different assumptions.

Where a selection is still open, ask how the amount will be confirmed. Tile supply cannot be compared fairly if one quote assumes owner-supplied tiles while another includes materials. The same applies to fixtures and concealed substrate repairs.

How do resealing, membrane renewal and full retiling quotes differ?

When is local resealing the quoted repair?

MEL ONE checks the seals, grout, drainage and adjoining surfaces on site. Where the assessment identifies an accessible joint repair, the resealing quote names the joints, preparation and finishing work. Membrane renewal, drainage correction and plumbing work are identified and quoted as their own scope.

What does a waterproofing membrane renewal quote cover?

The scope needs to explain how the existing membrane is reached and replaced, which surfaces and details are included, and what preparation is required. If retiling or fixture reinstatement is excluded, that exclusion affects the total cost of returning the area to use.

What does a full waterproofing and retiling quote cover?

A fuller project joins demolition, substrate preparation, waterproofing and finished surfaces. Confirm tile supply, flexible joints, fittings, waste and related trades so the room or balcony can be handed over with a clear record of completed and outstanding work.

Our guide to regrouting, resealing or rewaterproofing explains why the visible symptom alone does not decide the repair. Where water follows shower use, rain or a plumbing change, send that pattern with your enquiry so the inspection has a useful starting point.

How are concealed conditions and variations approved?

MEL ONE checks the accessible tile layers, substrate condition and previous repairs on site, then confirms the repair plan and written quote. If removal is needed to inspect a concealed area, we explain that access work and agree it with you before proceeding.

If removal exposes damage or a drainage issue, the sequence should be clear: explain the finding, describe the extra preparation or trade work, set out its cost and timing effect, then obtain written approval before proceeding with the additional scope. Keep the update with the original quotation. This prevents a changed project from being measured against an outdated list of inclusions.

When can waterproofing be covered by tiles?

The quote should name the selected system and confirm when it is ready for the next stage. Substrate condition, junctions and drainage details need attention as part of the construction, rather than being hidden inside a general “membrane” line.

The ABCB waterproofing FAQ explains that membrane position can differ with the applicable construction and compliance pathway. Existing-building requirements depend on state or territory rules. Confirm the design and project requirements for the ACT property; do not assume that a generic diagram settles the detail.

Product curing and testing also need a project-specific arrangement. As a product-specific example, the ARDEX technical bulletin on flood testing discusses cure readiness for its membranes. Your project's schedule follows the selected manufacturer's requirements and site conditions.

Ask what inspection or test is included, when the area may be covered, and who records that release. Where a flood test is specified, it must fit the selected product and project conditions. A homeowner should not fill a suspect bathroom or balcony to trial a test. Agree protection of completed waterproofing from damage or later penetrations before the tiler or other trades return.

How should room downtime and handover be planned?

Give MEL ONE the Canberra suburb, floor level, parking and entry arrangements. For an apartment, include lift bookings, permitted work hours, common-area protection and any strata approval requirements. Name the on-site contact and the person who approves changes.

Confirm room downtime before scheduling work: which facilities will be unavailable, when removal starts, and which curing, testing and finishing steps must be completed before use resumes. The period depends on the agreed work, products and site conditions. An alternative bathroom or arrangements for residents may affect how the work is booked.

Request a handover record identifying the area treated, agreed materials, relevant photos, inspection or test records where included, care and return-to-use instructions, and any remaining reinstatement tasks. Keep it with the quote and approved variations so future maintenance has a clear reference.

What else should you ask when comparing waterproofing quotes?

Is the lowest membrane price the lowest complete project price?

Not necessarily. Compare the same removal, preparation, materials, finishes and reinstatement. A membrane-only amount may leave substantial work to be arranged separately.

Should I choose the tiles before requesting a quote?

No. Contact MEL ONE before choosing tiles if you need help. We measure and check the site, discuss tile supply and selections, and confirm how size and thickness affect preparation, levels, laying work and the written quote.

Can the quote confirm all hidden damage from photographs?

No. Ask which conditions remain unverified and how any findings after removal will be priced and approved. Additional work should be explained before the scope expands.

Does every project include the same flood test?

No. Confirm the selected system, applicable project requirements, curing conditions and agreed testing or inspection. The quotation should describe what is included and the handover point before covering the work.

What if I cannot be at the property during the work?

Arrange entry, an on-site contact where needed and a reachable approval contact. Agree how findings, photographs and variations will be shared, along with room downtime and the return-to-use instructions.
