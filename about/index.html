Our waterproofing team

Waterproofing team and company background

MEL ONE is the trading name of Mel One Property Maintenance Pty Ltd, an Australian private company. The Ellis team brings more than 10 years of waterproofing experience and completes MEL ONE’s agreed work directly.

More than 10,000 customers across Australia have trusted MEL ONE for property maintenance and repair services.

Our local services cover wet-area waterproofing, leaking showers, balconies, external water ingress and roof leaks, alongside sealing and tile regrouting.

Waterproofing, sealing and grout repairs

Waterproofing and repair capabilities.

Wet-area and external waterproofing, sealing, retiling, balcony and general tile regrouting, including epoxy grout work, are available within the assessed scope.

  • Bathroom waterproofing
  • Leaking shower repairs
  • Shower resealing and regrouting
  • Balcony waterproofing
  • Kitchen sealing
  • Laundry waterproofing
  • External waterproofing
  • Roof waterproofing and leak repairs
  • Retaining-wall waterproofing
  • Tile regrouting and epoxy grout
  • Commercial waterproofing

Resealing and regrouting address joints and grout; they do not replace a failed waterproofing membrane. The on-site assessment determines the appropriate work.
