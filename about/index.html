How a Canberra waterproofing enquiry is arranged

A clear path from enquiry to next step.

01

Share the essentials

Send the suburb, affected area, when the issue occurs, useful photos and a preferred time for a response. Please do not include financial, identity or access information.

02

Confirm the condition

We review the information available and clarify practical details such as access, the building type, visible damage, previous work and whether specialist trade input may be required.

03

Agree the scope

Before work is booked, the quote or agreed documentation sets out inclusions, exclusions, responsible parties, timing and any licence information relevant to the work.

What helps a repair run well

The property conditions matter.

Access and daily use

Occupied rooms, tenants, pets, parking, lift access, noise limits and the spaces that must remain usable can affect staging.

Existing construction

Older alterations, concealed services, substrate movement, drainage falls and prior repairs may change what is found once the area is assessed.

Selections and sequencing

Tiles, fittings, membranes, sealants and made-to-order items need to align with the agreed sequence, drying times and specialist trade work.

Helpful information to send with an enquiry.

  • Your Canberra suburb or postcode and the property type.
  • Which room, surface or building detail is affected.
  • When water appears and whether it is linked to showers, rain, plumbing use or another event.
  • Wide photos plus close-ups of joints, drains, thresholds, cracked areas or staining where safe to take.
  • Any relevant prior repairs, reports, renovation plans or access constraints.

Good to know

Questions homeowners often ask.

What work can I enquire about?

We take Canberra enquiries for bathrooms, showers, tile and grout repairs, resealing, balconies, kitchens, laundries, external water ingress and roof leaks. The right scope is confirmed after reviewing the actual condition.

Will you diagnose a leak from photos alone?

Photos can make an enquiry more useful, but they may not show concealed water paths, substrate conditions or previous alterations. Further inspection or specialist input may be needed before a repair scope is confirmed.

Who performs regulated work?

For work that requires a specific licence or specialist trade, the quote identifies the responsible appropriately licensed contractor before the work proceeds.

What does the insurance information mean?

The public and products liability information is provided for transparency. Cover remains subject to the policy wording, period, conditions and insurer terms.
