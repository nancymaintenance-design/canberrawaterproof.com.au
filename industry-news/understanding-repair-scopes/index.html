Waterproofing repair quote inclusions

Our waterproofing quote lists the work needed for your property, including the agreed inspection, access, removal, preparation, membrane installation and finishing items. We distinguish included work from exclusions, such as separate plumbing or drainage tasks, before you approve the repair. Curing and the work sequence are confirmed for the selected system.

Comparing waterproofing and leak repair quotes

Compare the scope, not only the headline price. Check that each quote describes a similar area, access method, materials, preparation and reinstatement. Ask what is included if damaged substrate is uncovered, how variations are approved and whether fixtures need to be removed. A quote for local shower resealing is not the same scope as removing tiles and replacing a concealed membrane.

Plumbing, roof, drainage and waterproofing repair boundaries

We identify the water path and separate waterproofing from plumbing, roof-covering and stormwater-drainage work. Your quote identifies any other licensed trade needed, so a joint repair or membrane renewal is not mistaken for a pipe or drainage repair.

Waterproofing repair team and agreed scope

We carry out the agreed MEL ONE work directly. Before starting, we confirm the inclusions, access requirements, sequence, quote validity and exclusions in writing. We explain how the proposed method addresses the affected area and reported symptoms, including any concealed conditions that need further access before the repair can be confirmed.

For bathroom waterproofing and retiling, shower resealing and regrouting, or external water-ingress repairs, contact MEL ONE to discuss an on-site inspection and written waterproofing quote.
