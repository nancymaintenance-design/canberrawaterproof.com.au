Why a Clear Repair Scope Matters

Local sealing, membrane replacement and retiling are different work scopes with different inclusions.

What to look for

Start with observable information: the area affected, when it happens, any recent changes and the extent of visible moisture or deterioration. This helps frame an enquiry without treating the symptoms as a diagnosis.

Why the scope matters

Waterproofing, sealing, tiling, plumbing and drainage can involve different repair pathways. The practical next step depends on the source of entry and the existing construction, so the work should be confirmed before a solution is promised.

Prepare for a useful conversation

Include your Canberra suburb, affected area, timing and clear photos where available. A written scope should distinguish inspection, local repair, removal, waterproofing, retiling and finishing work.
