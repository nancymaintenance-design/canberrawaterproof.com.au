FAQ

Waterproofing FAQs.

Useful answers before you contact MEL ONE.

Do you service all Canberra suburbs?

Our service area is organised across Canberra’s nine districts. Tell us your suburb and type of work so coverage and availability can be confirmed.

Can I send photos before arranging a visit?

Yes. Photos help with the initial discussion but do not confirm the cause of a leak.

Can a leaking shower be repaired without removing tiles?

It depends on the source and condition of the surrounding work. Some local repairs may suit; other work requires access beneath tiles.

Is resealing the same as replacing waterproofing?

No. Resealing addresses specified joints or edges; membrane replacement is a different repair scope.

How much will waterproofing cost?

Cost depends on the affected area, access, removal, substrate condition, materials and finishing requirements.
