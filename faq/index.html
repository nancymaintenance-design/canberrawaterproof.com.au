Do you provide waterproofing services in all Canberra suburbs?

The Ellis team carries out MEL ONE waterproofing and leak-repair work across Canberra. Tell us your suburb, property type and affected area and our team will promptly outline the next practical step.

Can I send waterproofing photos before arranging a visit?

Yes. Photos are optional: email safely taken wide photos and close-ups of visible joints, drains, thresholds or staining to riley@melonemaintenance.com.au. They help us prepare but do not confirm a concealed leak source. You can arrange an on-site assessment without photos.

What information should a waterproofing quote include?

A useful quote should identify the affected area, inclusions, exclusions, removal and preparation work, waterproofing or sealing scope, finishes, access assumptions, the work the Ellis team completes, any separately licensed tasks and how variations will be handled. It should not imply that work outside the stated scope is included.

How should I prepare for a waterproofing inspection or repair?

Clear personal items from the affected area where practical, provide safe access information and keep relevant reports or photos available. Do not remove tiles, open walls or disconnect services unless advised by an appropriate professional.

Can a tenant report a waterproofing issue?

Yes. A tenant can document the symptom, dates, affected area and photos, then notify the landlord or property manager in line with their tenancy arrangements. Access, approval and repair decisions should be coordinated with the property owner or manager.

How much will waterproofing cost?

MEL ONE provides a clear quote for the affected area, access, preparation, materials, finishes and the agreed waterproofing scope, identifying any separate licensed task. A site-specific assessment is needed before a reliable price or timeframe can be confirmed.

What records should I discuss before bathroom waterproofing work?

Ask what written scope, material information, concealed-stage photos and completion records will be provided for the agreed work. Confirm any project-specific approval or certification requirements before work starts rather than assuming every repair has the same paperwork.

Does an ABN prove a waterproofing tradesperson holds the required licence?

No. Business registration and trade licensing are separate checks. Ask who is responsible for each task and check any relevant licence and insurance for that scope. Access Canberra explains how to check tradespeople and the relevant public registers.

Leak Symptoms & Repair Scenarios

Find a practical starting point for leaking showers, balcony ponding, damp cupboards and rainwater entry.

What are the common signs of a waterproofing problem?

Common signs include recurring mould, peeling paint, swollen skirting, loose tiles, stained ceilings below a wet area, damp carpet beside a bathroom, persistent musty odours and water appearing after showering or rain. These signs identify an area to investigate; they do not by themselves prove a failed membrane.

How can I tell whether a leak is plumbing or waterproofing?

A plumbing leak is often linked to water supply or drainage use, while a waterproofing issue can relate to shower spray, surface water, joints or rainfall. MEL ONE reviews the timing, location and accessible details to recommend the waterproofing scope and explains if a separately licensed task is also needed.

Can a leaking shower be repaired without removing tiles?

Sometimes. An accessible seal or joint fault can be repaired without tile removal; a failed concealed membrane or damaged substrate may require removal. MEL ONE checks the leak source and construction on site and confirms any access, repair and retiling work in the written quote.

Can a leak appear away from the water-entry point?

Yes. Water can follow falls, framing, services, cavities and layers beneath finishes before becoming visible elsewhere. The visible stain or mould may therefore be a symptom rather than the original point of entry.

Will mould go away once I reseal the shower?

Not necessarily. Mould needs safe cleaning and moisture control, while resealing only addresses a limited part of a possible moisture pathway. If mould returns, the source of moisture, ventilation and surrounding materials may need further investigation.

Can I keep using the bathroom while a leak is being investigated?

MEL ONE will explain the safest way to use and protect the area while the concern is assessed. Stop using the area where there is an electrical, slip, ceiling-collapse or significant water-entry risk.

Do you need a plumber for every wet-area leak?

No, but plumbing input is appropriate when the evidence points to supply pipes, wastes, fixtures or drainage. The Ellis team completes the agreed waterproofing work. If the repair also needs a separately licensed plumbing, electrical or building task, the quote names the licensed person responsible for it.

Can just the shower be rewaterproofed rather than the whole bathroom?

Sometimes, but the repair needs a sound substrate and a workable connection to the surrounding waterproofing. The assessment determines whether a shower-only scope is suitable; it does not automatically require a complete bathroom renovation.

Shower-only waterproofing and retiling

When does shower silicone replacement help?

Replacing deteriorated silicone can help where the issue is limited to an accessible movement joint or edge. Silicone renewal does not repair concealed membrane failure, drainage problems, damaged substrates or plumbing faults.

How is a balcony leak different from a shower leak?

A balcony leak can involve rain, drainage, door thresholds, flashing, external wall junctions, surface finishes or membrane details. A shower leak is usually influenced by internal wet-area use, fittings, drainage and enclosure details; the investigation approach should reflect those different water paths.

Why does water pool on a balcony after rain?

Falls, outlet condition and drainage capacity can contribute to ponding. A new coating alone does not correct every drainage or level problem. Describe where water collects and where it appears below, without entering unsafe areas.

Balcony leaks and ponding

Can resealing a kitchen sink fix a wet cupboard underneath?

Only if the water path is the affected surface joint. A leaking supply connection, waste or fitting needs a plumbing repair. Renewing a perimeter seal does not restore water-damaged cabinet material.

Kitchen sink sealing or plumbing

Can external water ingress be fixed with sealant alone?

Sometimes a local sealant repair is appropriate for a defined accessible joint, but sealant alone is not a universal solution. External water entry can involve drainage, flashing, cladding, cracks, membranes, roof details or adjacent construction.

Can a roof membrane be locally repaired instead of replaced?

That depends on the existing system, substrate condition, extent of damage and whether a compatible repair can connect to sound material. An isolated patch is not a sound choice merely because the visible stain is small.

Roof membrane assessment

How is condensation different from rainwater ingress?

Condensation can arise when moist air meets a cold surface; rainwater enters through an external path. Weather and use patterns help the investigation, but a stain alone cannot identify which is occurring.

External moisture assessment

Will exterior paint or another sealant layer fix water ingress?

Not necessarily. A vulnerable window junction, flashing, crack or drainage path may need a different repair. The treatment should address the confirmed entry path, not just cover the visible mark.

External water ingress repairs

Does water pooling beside a house need waterproofing or drainage?

If water is collecting against the building, its source and discharge path need assessment. Waterproofing protects the specified construction detail; it does not automatically remove ponding or replace drainage work.

Waterproofing or drainage

What should I do first if water is actively leaking?

Keep people clear of wet electrical equipment, significant flooding and unsafe ceilings. For immediate danger, move to safety and call 000. For urgent repair assistance, call MEL ONE with the location and visible water path so we can arrange assessment and the required qualified trades. Do not delay contact to take photos.

Waterproofing Knowledge & Materials

Understand membranes, grout, flexible joints, preparation and curing before choosing a repair scope.

What is the difference between waterproof and water resistant?

Waterproof means a building element is intended to resist the passage of water under the conditions required for that location; water resistant is a different level of protection. MEL ONE reviews the area, substrate, building classification and project scope to recommend the correct waterproofing detail for the job.

Do all bathroom floors need the same waterproofing treatment?

No. Waterproofing and water-resistance requirements can differ between shower areas, areas outside showers, baths, laundries and different floor types. Existing buildings, renovations and the proposed scope can also change what needs to be considered.

Why is the wall-to-floor junction important in a shower?

The wall-to-floor junction is a high-risk transition because water can collect and move through discontinuities at the change of plane. The detail must work with the waterproofing system, drainage falls, penetrations and finishes rather than being treated as a cosmetic sealant line only.

What should be checked before waterproofing over an existing surface?

Check the substrate condition, moisture, previous coatings, junctions and compatibility with the selected membrane. Cleaning, repair and levelling come before the waterproofing application; damaged backing is not corrected by covering it.

Bathroom substrate preparation

How long does waterproofing need to cure before tiling?

Use the selected system's instructions and actual site conditions. Tile-over time can differ from full cure and return-to-use time, especially at junctions. Temperature, moisture and coat thickness affect the schedule.

Membrane preparation and curing

Does cracked grout mean the waterproofing membrane has failed?

No. Cracked or missing grout can be a maintenance issue, but grout is not the waterproofing membrane. It can signal movement, wear or water entry at a joint, so the surrounding condition should be considered before deciding on regrouting or more extensive work.

Is resealing the same as replacing waterproofing?

No. Resealing addresses specified joints, edges or penetrations, while waterproofing replacement involves a different scope beneath the finished surface. A written scope should clearly state which work is included.

Is epoxy grout the same as an epoxy moisture barrier?

No. Epoxy tile grout is a joint-filling product for suitable tiles. An epoxy moisture barrier is a coating used on a substrate within a specified system. Neither name means it replaces every part of a waterproofing assembly.

Tile grout and material selection

What is a bond breaker, and is it tile grout?

A bond breaker controls adhesion at a specified joint or membrane detail so movement can be accommodated. It is not grout for filling fixed tile joints. The selected waterproofing system determines the required joint treatment.

Joint sealing and bond breakers

Can surface sealing replace a balcony waterproofing membrane?

No. A surface treatment or local joint repair has a different role from the membrane beneath finishes. Water reaching a room below may require investigation of the membrane, thresholds, flashings and drainage before choosing the repair.

Balcony repair options

Is ground-level paving the same as a balcony over a room?

No. An elevated balcony may form part of the weatherproof enclosure over occupied space, with thresholds and membrane junctions to consider. Ground-level paving can involve a different drainage and construction problem.

Balcony assessment

What needs checking before laundry floor tiling?

Review the substrate, drainage, penetrations and floor-to-wall junctions before they are covered. Confirm the waterproofing scope for the particular floor and room rather than assuming the bathroom detail applies unchanged.

Laundry floor waterproofing

Is roof painting the same as roof waterproofing?

No. Roof paint is not automatically a repair for a failed membrane, flashing, penetration or outlet detail. The water path and existing roof assembly determine whether waterproofing, roofing or drainage work is appropriate.

Roof waterproofing and the Isaacs project
